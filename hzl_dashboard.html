How the main dashboard values are calculated

RR = RR weight, Recv = received weight, GCV ARB / ADB = grades from the record. Sums are for the selected quarter.

  • Performance on GCV = sum of (RR × GCV ARB) ÷ sum of RR. Position of GCV for Achieve Target = (sum of (RR × GCV ADB) ÷ sum of RR) − Performance on GCV
  • Shortage / Excess % = (sum of Recv − sum of RR) ÷ sum of RR × 100; MT per rake = (sum of Recv − sum of RR) ÷ number of rakes
  • Quantity Reco = rate from the shortage table × sum of Recv; GCV Reco = sum of bonus_penalty from the database
  • Service Charge SECL / CCL / MCL = area sum of Recv × fixed service charge (Data Entry)
  • 5% of Total Railway Penalty = Total Penalty × penalty share % (Formulas); Total Penalty = Overload + Punitive + EHBC + ENHC (Dead Freight is shown but NOT included)
  • TOTAL BILLING = Quantity Reco + GCV Reco + Service SECL + Service CCL + Service MCL − 5% penalty
  • Expected Billing of the Qtr = TOTAL BILLING ÷ days passed in the quarter × total days in the quarter
  • Area Target, Loaded Till, Balance and Asking Per Day come from the Target Rake table in Data Entry
  • Mines are matched by siding name (GOVIND, KMKA, NKCR, OKSR, GPCK, JRGR, DIPKA, SLOG); CCL and MCL use the area field
  • Mines Loading Duration = P/T to C/T time difference; Sick Box = Supply Box − Load Box; Underload Quantity = Ch Weight − RR Weight
