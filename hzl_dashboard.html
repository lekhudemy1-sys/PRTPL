Formulas

Change the calculation rules here. The dashboard uses the new values as soon as you save.

Shortage / Excess Rate

Each row says: if the shortage / excess % is up to this limit, the rate per MT is applied. Rows are checked from top to bottom, so keep the limits in increasing order.

Other Rules

Target = rakes placed × this value
Applied to Total Penalty for the "% of" lines on the dashboard

How every dashboard value is calculated

Use this list to trace a wrong value. RR = RR weight (rr_wt), Recv = received weight (recv_wt), Qtr = the selected quarter. "Sum" means the total for the quarter, unless a mine or area filter is named.

Key Performance (top left card)

  • Per Day Required Rake for Target = sum of "Asking Per Day" from the Target Rake table (Data Entry)
  • Performance on Rake Materialization = sum of "Asking (%)" from the Target Rake table
  • Performance on GCV (SECL/CCL/MCL) = sum of (RR × GCV ARB) ÷ sum of RR
  • Position of GCV for Achieve Target = (sum of (RR × GCV ADB) ÷ sum of RR) − Performance on GCV
  • Position of GCV for SECL / CCL / MCL Rakes = that area's sum of GCV ADB − sum of GCV ARB

Area Targets (Qtr)

  • Target of Qtr = sum of Qtr Target for the area (Data Entry)
  • Rake Loaded Till = count of area rakes dated up to the report date (or quarter end if no report date)
  • Balance Rake = Target of Qtr − Rake Loaded Till
  • Asking Per Day = sum of "Asking Per Day" for the area from the Target Rake table

Shortage / Excess Coal

  • Avg Shrt / Excess (%) = (sum of Recv − sum of RR) ÷ number of rakes (the code gives MT per rake, not %)
  • Shortage / Excess MT per rake = (sum of Recv − sum of RR) ÷ sum of RR × 100 (the code gives %, not MT)
  • Avg. Wt. GCV (per area) = simple average of GCV ARB for the area's rakes
  • Per GCV Cost (per area) = sum of Landed Cost ÷ sum of GCV ARB for the area

Billing Performance

  • Rate per MT (used below) = band rate from the Formulas table, chosen by "Shortage / Excess MT per rake" value
  • Performance on Quantity Reco = rate per MT × sum of RR; the per-MT box shows the rate
  • Performance on GCV Reco = sum of bonus_penalty from the database; per-MT box = that sum ÷ sum of RR
  • Service Charge SECL / CCL / MCL = area sum of RR × fixed service charge (Data Entry); per-MT box = charge ÷ area sum of RR
  • 5% of Total Railway Penalty = Total Penalty × penalty share % (Formulas); per-MT box = ÷ sum of RR
  • TOTAL BILLING = Quantity Reco + GCV Reco + Service SECL + Service CCL + Service MCL − 5% penalty (the 5% line is negative, shown red); per-MT box = ÷ sum of RR
  • Expected Billing of the Qtr = TOTAL BILLING ÷ days passed in the quarter × total days in the quarter

Rake Performance

  • Per Day Rakes – Achieve = number of rakes ÷ total days in the quarter; Target = Per Day Required Rake
  • Per Day Quantity – Achieve = sum of RR ÷ total days in the quarter; Target = number of rakes × MT per rake (Formulas)
  • Per GCV Cost – Achieve = SECL Per GCV Cost; Target = Per GCV Cost Target (Data Entry)
  • Avg. CH.Wt. = average Ch Weight; Avg. RR.Wt. = average RR Weight; Avg. Plant Rec. Wt. = average Recv
  • Average GCV = average GCV ADB of the rakes
  • Total RR Weight = sum of RR; Total Plant Receive = sum of Recv

Railway Freight Penalties

  • Underload Charge = sum of ul_ch (Data Entry) for the quarter; per-MT box = charge ÷ sum of RR
  • Overload Charge = sum of ol_ch (Data Entry) for the quarter; per-MT box = charge ÷ sum of RR
  • Punitive Sum = sum of POL group charges; EHBC / FAUC / TC = sum of EHBC group; ENHC = sum of ENHC charge; Dead Freight = sum of dead freight charges
  • TOTAL PENALTY = Overload + Punitive + EHBC + ENHC (Dead Freight is shown in grey and is NOT included); per-MT box = ÷ sum of RR
  • 5% of Total Penalty = TOTAL PENALTY × penalty share % (Formulas)

Mines Wise Performance (one column per mine)

  • Mine rows are matched by siding name (for example, a siding containing KMKA counts for KMKA); CCL and MCL use the area field
  • GCV (ARB) = sum of (RR × GCV ARB) ÷ sum of RR for the mine
  • Quality Diff. ADB/ARB = (sum of GCV ADB × RR ÷ sum of RR) − (sum of GCV ARB × RR ÷ sum of RR) for the mine
  • Performance on GCV Mines Wise = sum of bonus_penalty for the mine
  • Cost Rs. GCV Per Paisa = sum of Landed Cost ÷ sum of GCV ARB
  • Shortage = sum of Recv − sum of RR
  • Punitive, EHBC and ENHC rows = sums of the matching freight columns

Monthly & Quarterly Performance

  • Rs. Per GCV Cost (month) = sum of Landed Cost ÷ sum of GCV ARB for that month
  • Per Day Qty (month) = sum of RR for the month ÷ days elapsed in that month
  • WT. GCV (month) = sum of (RR × GCV ARB) ÷ sum of RR for the month
  • Quarterly values repeat the same formulas across the whole quarter; Quarterly Billing = that quarter's TOTAL BILLING
  • % = number of rakes ÷ quarter target × 100 (capped at 100%; shown red when below 100%)

Calculated in Data Entry and the database

  • Mines Loading Duration = P/T to C/T time difference, shown as HH:MM hrs
  • Sick Box = Supply Box − Load Box
  • Bonus penalty (bonus_penalty, gcv_bonus_penalty_per_mt) = calculated by the database trigger on insert/update of grade, GCV ARB or RR weight; the dashboard only reads it
