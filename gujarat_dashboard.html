Formulas

Change the calculation rules here. The dashboard uses the new values as soon as you save.

Quantity Reco: Materialisation Tiers

Each row says: if rake materialisation is at least this %, the rate is changed by this % of the base rate. Rows are checked from top to bottom, so keep the limits in decreasing order. Below the last row the "below" rate applies.

GCV Reco: Grade Slippage

Slippage = unloading grade minus loading grade. Each row is the % of the GCV base rate paid or deducted per MT of RR weight. Negative slippage pays nothing. The database recalculates every record when you save new values here.

How every dashboard value is calculated

RR = RR weight, Recv = received weight, GETS / TPHS = party type on the record. "Month" means the selected month. Materialisation uses rakes loaded up to the report date.

Plants and Weights

  • Target of Month, Rake Loaded Till, Balance Rake, Asking Per Day = per party (GETS, TPHS) and area (SECL, WCL) from Data Entry targets; loaded counts rakes up to the report date; asking per day = balance ÷ days from report date to the 3rd of the next month
  • Avg. CH.Wt., Avg. RR.Wt., Avg. Plant Rec. Wt. = simple averages of the rakes; TOTAL RR WEIGHT = RR of rakes with received weight; PLANT RECEIVE = sum of received weight

Rake Performance

  • GETS / TPHS achieve = materialisation (loaded ÷ target × 100) against 100%; Rakes per day = total loaded ÷ days passed, against total asking per day

Billing

  • Materialisation % (GETS, TPHS) = loaded ÷ target × 100; tier % comes from the tiers above
  • Quantity Reco = Σ over party of (Quantity base rate × tier %) × RR weight of that party's rakes
  • Grade Slippage = Unloading Grade − Loading Grade (both read from the record). GCV Bonus/Penalty (₹/MT) = GCV Base Rate × slippage % ÷ 100. Bonus/Penalty (₹) = RR weight × that ₹/MT. The database calculates these three whenever a record is saved or the base rate / slippage % change; the page only reads them.
  • Performance on GCV Reco = sum of the saved Bonus/Penalty (₹) of the month's rakes. Slippage 0 = +2%; 1 = −5%; 2 = −40%; 3 = −60%; above 3 = −100%. Blank or negative slippage is excluded.
  • Service Charge SECL / WCL = RR weight of that area × rate (Data Entry)
  • Total Railway Penalty = Underload + Overload + Punitive + EHBC/FAUC/SD/TC + ENHC + Dead Freight (typed per month in Data Entry)
  • TOTAL BILLING = Quantity Reco + GCV Reco + Service SECL + Service WCL − Total Railway Penalty. Per MT: quantity reco ÷ plant receive, GCV reco ÷ RR weight of rakes with non-negative slippage, service ÷ area RR, penalty ÷ total RR; TOTAL per MT = quantity + GCV + average of the two service per MT + penalty per MT (as in the workbook)
  • Expected Billing = TOTAL BILLING ÷ days passed in month × days in month

Shortage / Excess

  • Shortage % = (sum RR − sum Recv) × 100 ÷ sum RR, over rakes with received weight (positive = shortage)
  • Shortage MT per rake = (sum RR − sum Recv) ÷ number of rakes with received weight
  • Grade Difference (SECL / WCL) = Unloading Grade − Loading Grade, shown as a number

Mines Wise (matched by siding code)

  • GCV = RR-weighted GCV ARB; Quantity = sum RR; penalties = sums of the matching charge columns; Cost per MT = penalties ÷ RR of that mine
