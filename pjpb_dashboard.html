Quarter

Area targets (rakes in the quarter)

Service charge (quarter)

Window: 4th of the first month to 3rd of the month after the quarter. Materialization = rakes loaded from the log × 100 ÷ total rakes; the factor is the first band where it is below the limit. Service charge = total RR weight × factor × multiplier.

Performance for SECL (quantity reco)

Materialization comes from the service charge box. Factor: above 90 → 0.5, above 80 → 0.25, otherwise 0; rate = factor × rate multiplier. Incentive = rate × total RR weight (MT).

GCV performance (auto from log, quarter)

Weighted GCV = Σ(GCV × RR wt) ÷ Σ RR wt, per area and month. Difference = loading-end − plant-end. Less 200 kcal. 20% per 100 kcal, max 75%. Rate = − % × 4.73 per MT. Amount = rate × RR wt.
