How the numbers work. Loaded = Loaded up to the previous day + Today loading. Balance = Target − Loaded. Asking rakes per day (P/D) = Balance ÷ (Days in month − day of the report date). Pick a date to open that day's entry. A new date starts with the targets and the loaded total from the latest earlier entry of the same month, and Today loading is left empty so you only type what was loaded today. Save entry stores the day in Supabase; saving the same date again updates it. Ctrl+S also saves.
