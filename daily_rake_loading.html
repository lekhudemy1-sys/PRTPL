How the numbers work. Balance = Target − Loaded. Asking rakes per day (P/D) = Balance ÷ (Days in month − day of the report date). Pick a date to open that day's entry. A new date starts with the full data (targets + loaded) from the latest earlier entry of the same month so you can see the previous day and only update what changed.
