Quantity Reco: Materialisation Tiers

If rake materialisation is at least this %, the Quantity Reco rate changes by this % of the base rate. Keep the limits in decreasing order. Below the last row the "below" rate applies.

GCV Reco: Grade Slippage

Slippage = unloading grade minus loading grade. Each row is the % of the GCV base rate paid or deducted per MT of RR weight. Negative slippage pays nothing.

How every dashboard value is calculated

Contract period is 10 Oct to 13 Nov 2026 (35 days). RR = RR weight, Recv = received weight.

  • Target of Contract, Rake Loaded Till, Balance Rake, Asking Per Day = per area (TALCHER, IB, CKP). Loaded counts rakes up to the report date. Asking per day = balance ÷ days from today to the 3rd of next month.
  • Materialisation % = total RR Qty ÷ 97,000 × 100. Rake Performance shows achieved RR Qty against the 97,000 quantity target. Quantity Reco = total RR weight × Quantity base rate × tier % (tiers above).
  • Grade of a rake comes from its GCV: above 7000 = G-01, above 6700 = G-02, then every 300 lower (G-17 above 2200). Grade Slippage = Unloading Grade − Loading Grade. GCV Bonus/Penalty (₹/MT) = GCV base rate × slippage % ÷ 100. Bonus/Penalty (₹) = RR weight × that ₹/MT.
  • Performance on GCV Reco = sum of Bonus/Penalty (₹) of the month's rakes. Rakes with a missing GCV or negative slippage are left out.
  • Service Charge (each area) = RR weight of that area × service rate (Data Entry).
  • Total Railway Penalty = Underload + Overload + Punitive (POL) + EHBC/FAUC/TC + ENHC + Dead Freight (typed per contract).
  • TOTAL BILLING = Quantity Reco + GCV Reco + Service Charges − Total Railway Penalty. Per MT = amount ÷ total RR weight (GCV Reco ÷ RR weight of rakes counted). Expected Billing = TOTAL BILLING (contract is closed, so expected billing equals achieved billing).
  • Shortage % = (RR − Recv) × 100 ÷ RR over rakes with received weight. Shortage MT per rake = (RR − Recv) ÷ number of those rakes.
  • Area Wise: GCV = RR-weighted GCV; Quality Diff. = Loading GCV − Unloading GCV; Per Ton Cost = (POL + EHBC + ENHC) ÷ RR of that area.
