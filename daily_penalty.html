Rows marked LIVE are the GUJRAT POWER (Gujarat dashboard) and HZL (HZL dashboard) rakes read live, grouped by area and siding for the date, and refreshed every 30 seconds. For each live rake: Underload qty = CH weight − RR weight (when CH is more), Overload qty = RR weight − CH weight (when RR is more). Live rows can be edited here too (pencil button, or click RR QTY, CHARBALE QTY, Underload Penalty, Overload Penalty or ENCH). A hand-edited row is marked EDITED, is saved for that date only, is never overwritten by the live refresh, and does not change the Gujarat or HZL dashboard. The arrow button on an EDITED row goes back to the live values. All other rows come from the database: click RR QTY, CHARBALE QTY, Freight or ENCH to change them in place. Per M/T = TOTAL ÷ RR QTY.
